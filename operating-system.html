Closed-loop value network

Not a linear supply chain. A controlled agri-tech operating system.

KRS transforms the traditional Farmer -> Middleman -> Retailer -> Consumer chain into a closed-loop value network where consumer demand data flows back to farmers as fast as produce flows forward.

India's First Closed-Loop Agritech Value Network Empowering Farmers, Preserving Freshness, Building Wealth.
Seven-stage operation

The KRS model controls the full journey from input supply to consumer feedback.

01

Input Supply

Certified seeds, quality fertilizers, bio-inputs, and bulk procurement advantages for farmers.

02

Production

Precision agriculture, climate-resilient practices, soil health, water management, and crop rotation guidance.

03

Post-Harvest

Immediate pre-cooling, sorting, grading, and quality categorization to protect shelf life.

04

Processing

Commodity-specific packaging and handling that reduces transit damage and preserves freshness.

05

Market Access

Digital integration, contract farming, fixed MSP, and transparent procurement agreements.

06

Distribution

GPS-optimized routing, last-mile connectivity, and cold-chain logistics across cluster zones.

07

Retail & Consumers

Branded outlets, QR traceability, consumer trust, and feedback loops that inform the next planting cycle.

Three control levers

Controlled production, strategic storage, and on-time transportation reconnect the value chain.

Controlled Production

Synchronizes input supply and planting schedules with demand data from retailers and consumers, preventing blind growing and mandi gluts.

Strategic Storage

Village-level cold clusters buffer produce between post-harvest and processing, enabling value addition and shortage-period release.

On-Time Transportation

GPS-monitored cold-chain logistics preserve the quality created at farm level until it reaches the final consumer.
