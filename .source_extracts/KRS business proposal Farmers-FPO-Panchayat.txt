1. The KRS Promise: "Growing with Certainty" 
Traditionally, you take all the risk —you plant, you labor, and then you pray for a fair price at the 
Mandi. Kishan Retail Sathi (KRS)  changes this. We become your partner from  the day you prepare 
your land until the day your produce reaches the consumer’s plate. 
 
2. Our 6-Point Support System 
I. Guaranteed Price (Fixed MSP) 
 No More Distress Sales:  We offer a  Fixed Minimum Support Price (MSP)  signed in a long -
term contract before you even sow the seeds. 
 Market Intelligence: We tell you  what to grow based on real -time demand, ensuring your 
crop is sold before it is harvested. 
II. Input & Technical Training 
 Quality Seeds & Fertilizers: Access to high-yield, quality-tested seeds and bio-fertilizers. 
 Modern Techniques:  Training on soil health, water management, and pest control to 
increase your yield per acre. 
 Land Management:  Professional guidance on crop rotation to keep your land fertile for 
generations. 
III. Cold Storage & Zero Waste 
 Village-Level Clusters: You no longer have to rush to the Mandi to prevent rot. Use our local  
Cold Storage Clusters to keep your produce fresh. 
 Solar-Powered Solutions:  Through our partnership with experts like  Ecofrost, we provide 
reliable storage even in areas with power issues.
