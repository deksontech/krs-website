The  Integrated  Business  Model  Structure  This  model  functions  as  a  Closed-Loop  System ,  where  the  three  "control  areas"  act  as  the  
connective
 
tissue
 
between
 
the
 
seven
 
stages.
 
Control  Area  Primary  Function  Reconnection  Point  
Controlled  Production  
Synchronizes  Input  Supply  with  Production .  
Ensures  that  the  volume  grown  matches  the  demand  data  received  from  Retail/Consumers .  
Strategic  Storage  Acts  as  a  buffer  between  Post-Harvest  and  Processing .  
Prevents  market  gluts;  allows  the  business  to  release  product  during  high-demand  periods  (Value  Addition).  
On-Time  Transportation  
Bridges  Distribution  and  Market  Access .  
Maintains  the  "Cold  Chain"  integrity,  ensuring  that  the  quality  produced  at  the  farm  actually  reaches  the  consumer.  
How  it  Reconnects  The  model  creates  a  Feedback-Driven  Loop :  
1.  Demand  Signal:  Retailers  and  Consumers  provide  data  on  what  they  want.  2.  Controlled  Production:  The  production  stage  adjusts  inputs  and  planting  schedules
