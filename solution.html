The KRS solution

A vertically integrated farm-to-shelf supply chain with price guarantee, cold storage, and zero middlemen.

KRS applies an MSP-backed procurement system to vegetables, modelled on the trust of grain MSP and redesigned for perishables.

01

Farmer harvests

Enrolled farmers harvest produce with a declared MSP safety net.

02

KRS procures at MSP

Farmers receive MSP or above, with payments tracked digitally.

03

Cold storage

KRS-owned and operated cold rooms protect produce from wastage.

04

Smart release

Stored produce is released during shortage periods to reduce price shocks.

05

Branded retail outlet

KRS outlets deliver fresh vegetables directly to consumers.

No loss guarantee

Every farmer receives MSP or above. When KRS sells above MSP, farmers can receive bonus payments from the surplus.

Price stability for consumers

The cold storage model is designed to keep vegetable price swings within a Rs. 10-15/kg band across enrolled produce categories.

Zero middlemen

The direct farm-to-outlet chain redirects saved margins toward farmers, consumers and the operating system.
