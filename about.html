About KRS

India's first technology-enabled vegetable MSP platform.

Kissan Retail Sathi Pvt. Ltd. is building a direct farm-to-shelf supply chain where farmers receive guaranteed minimum prices and consumers receive price-stable fresh produce year-round.

Founding Vision

To build a system where every farmer gets a guaranteed minimum price and every consumer gets fresh produce without distress, scarcity, or middlemen.

Mission and vision

A farmer-first company profile built for district pilots, institutional partners, and national scale.

Mission

Protect farmers from distress sales through guaranteed procurement, cold-chain access, transparent payments, and direct retail demand.

Vision

Build India's trusted closed-loop vegetable value network where farmers earn fairly and consumers receive fresh produce at stable prices.

Operating Principle

Every rupee saved from middlemen is redirected into farmer protection, freshness preservation, and branded retail scale.
