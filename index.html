Control levers

Three operating controls make the model practical at district scale.

Controlled Production

Synchronizes input supply and planting schedules with market demand to reduce blind growing and mandi gluts.

Strategic Storage

Village-level cold clusters buffer produce between harvest and retail release, reducing wastage and scarcity.

On-Time Transportation

GPS-aligned logistics preserve farm-level quality until produce reaches KRS outlets and consumers.
