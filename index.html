The problem we solve

Three failures create one broken market: distress sales, wastage, and inflated retail prices.

₹₹3/kg

Farmer Distress Sales

Farmers are forced to sell onions, tomatoes and potatoes at harvest lows of ₹3-5/kg without MSP or an alternative buyer.

%40%

Post-Harvest Wastage

Fresh produce is lost after harvest because cold storage access is limited, creating later scarcity and price spikes.

x5-7x

Middlemen Exploitation

Multiple layers of intermediaries reduce farmer share while consumers still pay peak retail rates.
