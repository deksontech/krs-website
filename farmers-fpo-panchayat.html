KRS Sathi Program

Growing with certainty for farmers, FPOs, and Panchayats.

KRS positions farmers as Sathis, not vendors. The model is designed to increase farmer income from 25% to 65% of the consumer price through fixed MSP, market intelligence, cold storage, and direct digital payments.

Apna Haath, KRS Ka SaathDesh Ka Vikas, Kisan Ke Paas.
Six-point support system

KRS supports the farmer before sowing, during production, and after harvest.

01

Guaranteed Price

Fixed MSP contracts signed before sowing reduce distress sales and price uncertainty.

02

Input & Technical Training

Quality seeds, bio-fertilizers, soil health, water management, pest control, and crop rotation guidance.

03

Cold Storage & Zero Waste

Village-level cold clusters and solar-powered support help farmers avoid forced mandi sales.

04

Digital Onboarding

Simple local-language app onboarding with land details, crop details, and bank linkage.

05

Government Scheme Integration

Sathi representatives help farmers identify and apply for PM-KISAN, crop insurance, irrigation, and state schemes.

06

Professional Skilling

Training workshops and campaigns help farmers become professional agri-entrepreneurs.
